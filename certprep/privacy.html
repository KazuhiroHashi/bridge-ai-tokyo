Privacy Policy

Contents

    Last updated: October 17, 2026

    This policy applies to the app "AI & Cloud Cert Practice" (the "App") provided by Bridge AI. The App is free and shows ads.

    1. Information the developer collects

    The developer (Bridge AI) does not collect or store your personal information.

    1. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or address.
    2. There is no account registration or sign-in.
    3. The App does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos.
    4. The developer runs no servers, and no information about you is sent to the developer.

    2. Information stored on your device

    So that you can continue studying, the App stores the following only on your device. It is never sent off the device.

    1. Best scores and score history for each exam and practice test
    2. Answers and remaining time for a test in progress (to resume it)
    3. Questions you missed (for review) and questions you bookmarked
    4. Display settings such as light or dark mode
    5. A count of answered questions, used to decide when to show an ad

    This data is deleted when you delete the App or clear its data. The developer cannot see it.

    3. Ads

    The App uses Google AdMob, an advertising service of Google LLC. To show and improve ads, AdMob may collect information such as:

    1. Advertising identifiers (on iOS, the IDFA only if you allow tracking; on Android, the advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your device settings)
    2. Approximate location estimated from your IP address
    3. Device information such as model, OS version, and language
    4. Interactions with ads, such as impressions and taps
    5. Diagnostic information such as crashes and performance

    This information is collected and managed by Google under Google's policies; the developer does not receive it.

    1. How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
    2. Google Privacy Policy

    4. Tracking permission and personalized ads

    On iOS, the App asks on first launch whether you allow tracking for personalized ads (App Tracking Transparency). If you do not allow it, every feature still works, and ads will not be based on tracking. You can change this later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

    On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads (on some devices, Settings → Google → Ads). In regions where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the App shows Google's consent form (UMP) before showing ads.

    5. Network use

    All questions are included in the App, and studying works offline. The App uses the network only to load and show ads. When you are offline, no ads are shown and the study features still work.

    6. Children's privacy

    The App is designed for adults preparing for professional certifications. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

    7. About the questions

    The App is an unofficial study aid. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Anthropic, PBC, Amazon Web Services, Inc., or NVIDIA Corporation. Exam names are trademarks of their respective owners. All questions are original practice questions written for the App; they are not real exam questions.

    8. Changes to this policy

    If we change this policy, we will update this page and its "Last updated" date.
