    FAQ

    Is the app free?

    Yes. Every exam and every question is free. The app shows a full-screen ad once every 20 answered questions. There are no in-app purchases.

    When are ads shown?

    Once every 20 answered questions, when you tap Next or Score and the screen changes. Ads are never shown while you are reading a question or an explanation. In exam mode, the timer pauses while an ad is shown.

    Which exams are included?

    Practice tests for CCAO-F (Claude Certified Associate – Foundations), AWS Certified AI Practitioner (AIF-C01), and NVIDIA certifications (NCA-AIIO, NCA-GENL, NCA-GENM, NCP-GENL, NCP-AAI). The app store listing shows the current number of questions.

    The app is in Japanese

    The app follows your device language. On devices set to Japanese, it shows the Japanese AWS Certified AI Practitioner practice tests for users of our earlier Japanese app. On devices set to any other language, it shows the English practice tests.

    Does it work offline?

    Yes. All questions are built into the app, so every study feature works in airplane mode. Ads are not shown while you are offline.

    What is the difference between practice mode and exam mode?

    Practice mode checks each answer right away and shows why every option is right or wrong. Exam mode is timed: you answer the whole test, and it is scored at the end with your score by domain.

    I lost my study records after changing devices

    Scores, missed questions and bookmarks are stored only on your device. We do not collect personal information, and your records do not leave the device, so they do not transfer to a new device.

    I found an error in a question

    Please tell us. It helps if you include:

    • The exam name, practice test name and question number
    • What you think is wrong and why

    We will check it and fix it in an app update.

    Are these official exam questions?

    No. This is an unofficial study app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Anthropic, PBC, Amazon Web Services, Inc., or NVIDIA Corporation. Exam names are trademarks of their respective owners.

    All questions are original practice questions written for this app with the help of AI and reviewed one by one. They are not real exam questions. Exam content and product features change over time, so also check the official exam guides.
