Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-21

This privacy policy applies to the following mobile applications (each, the "App"), published by Bridge AI for iOS and Android:

  • Prep for NCA-AIIO
  • Prep for NCA-GENL
  • Prep for NCA-GENM
  • Prep for NCP-AAI

The four apps are built from the same code and behave identically in every respect described below. They differ only in which exam's questions they contain.

1. We collect nothing

The Apps do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or usage data.

Each App contains:

  • no user accounts and no sign-in of any kind
  • no analytics or crash-reporting SDK
  • no advertising SDK and no advertising identifier
  • no tracking across apps or websites
  • no contact form, no email collection, no push notifications

The developer receives no information about you from the Apps. We do not know who you are, that you installed an App, or how you use it.

2. The Apps do not use the network

Every question and every explanation is bundled inside the App at download time. An App makes no network requests while you use it, and it works with the device in airplane mode. Because nothing is sent anywhere, there is nothing to intercept, store, or leak.

No App requests any runtime permission: no camera, no microphone, no location, no contacts, no files.

3. What is stored on your device

An App saves your study progress on the device only:

  • which practice sets you have started or finished
  • your answers, scores and per-domain results for those sets
  • the questions you answered incorrectly and the ones you bookmarked
  • settings such as light or dark appearance

This data is written to the App's own private storage on your device using the operating system's standard local storage. It is:

  • never uploaded to any server
  • never sent to the developer
  • never shared with any third party
  • not included in any analytics, because there is no analytics

You can erase it at any time from the App's Settings screen with "Reset all progress". It is also removed when you delete the App. If your device is backed up to iCloud or to Google's backup service, the App's local data may be included in that backup. That backup is controlled entirely by you and by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies — the developer has no access to it.

Because progress is stored on the device only, it does not sync between your devices.

4. Purchases

Each App is a one-time paid download. It contains no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. Payment is handled entirely by the App Store or by Google Play. The developer never sees your name, your address, or your payment details. Sales reports from Apple and Google contain only aggregated, anonymous totals.

5. Third parties

No data is shared with third parties, because no data is collected. The Apps embed no third-party SDK.

6. Children

The Apps are suitable for all ages and collect no information from anyone, including children under 13. They show no advertising and contain no links out to other services.

7. Your rights

Because the Apps hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf. You can erase everything an App stores by using "Reset all progress" or by deleting the App from your device. Requests under GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA, or Japan's APPI can therefore be answered directly: we hold no data about you.

8. If you email us

The Apps themselves send nothing. If you choose to write to the support address below, we will have your email address and whatever you put in your message, for as long as it takes to answer you. That is ordinary email between you and the developer; it is not collected by any App and it is never used for marketing.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date.
