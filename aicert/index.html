Why four apps and not one

Each exam has its own question bank, its own domain list and its own way of asking. Mixing four of them into one download would mean paying for three exams you are not sitting, and searching the store for your exam would not find it. So each app carries one bank, one icon and one colour, and you buy only the one you need.

The same promises in all four

  • One-time purchase. No in-app purchases, no subscription, no locked sets
  • Everything is unlocked from the first launch — there is nothing to earn or wait for
  • Completely offline. The apps make no network requests at all, in any screen
  • No account, no sign-in, no email address, no push notifications
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifier
  • An explanation for every answer option, not only for the correct one
  • Exam mode with a timer, and practice mode that answers as you go
  • Automatic lists of the questions you missed and the ones you bookmarked
  • A score breakdown by domain after every set, so the weak area is obvious
  • Light and dark themes, or follow the system
  • Your progress is written to the device only, and it is deleted with the app

About the questions

The questions and the explanations in all four apps are written with AI, then organised against the domains each certification publishes. They are original practice material. They are not real exam questions, and no exam question is reproduced in them. AI-written material can be wrong: if something looks off, write to us and the correction ships in the next update for everybody.

Who makes these

Bridge AI is a one-person studio in Tokyo. The person behind it has passed all four of these exams, plus NCP-GENL, and holds more than thirty IT certifications. The apps are the study material that was missing while sitting them — something to work through on a commute with no signal.
